Bobolino is a colorful educational game designed to make early learning enjoyable for young children.

If you have questions, experience technical problems, or would like to share feedback, we're happy to help.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start playing Bobolino?

Open the app, select a learning activity, and follow the instructions on the screen.

Does Bobolino work offline?

Core learning activities are designed to work without an internet connection.

How is learning progress saved?

Learning progress is stored locally on your device.

What should I do if the app stops working?

Close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, contact our support team.

How can I report a problem?

Send an email describing the issue, your device model, and your app version.
