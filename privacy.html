Bobolino Privacy Policy

Protecting little learners and their families

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Publication note: This policy is a draft pending final verification of the production application's data practices. It must be reviewed before public release.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bobolino!

Bobolino is an educational mobile application designed for young children and their families.

We respect children's privacy and aim to provide a safe, age-appropriate learning experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using Bobolino.

2. Learning Progress

Bobolino is designed to save learning progress, including completed levels and earned stars, locally on the user's device.

This allows children to continue their learning activities when they reopen the app.

Local progress may be lost if the application is deleted or its stored data is cleared.

3. Children's Personal Information

Bobolino is designed to allow children to play without creating a personal account.

The educational activities do not require children to provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses.

Any information voluntarily sent to our support email is handled separately from children's gameplay.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Bobolino's current game design does not include advertising features or personalized advertising.

We do not intend to use children's information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Any future introduction of analytics, advertising, or additional third-party services will require a review and update of this policy.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

Bobolino's core educational activities are designed to work offline.

Certain parent-facing functions or platform services may require an internet connection.

Before public release, we will verify whether any production software components transmit diagnostic, technical, or other information to third-party services.

6. Parent-Focused Features

Bobolino includes a Parent Gate intended to separate parent-focused functions from children's learning activities.

Parents and guardians should supervise children when accessing external websites or platform account settings.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Learning progress stored locally may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

For questions about information voluntarily submitted to our support team, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Security

We aim to minimize the information required to use Bobolino and maintain appropriate safeguards for any information we handle.

No digital system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
